**BgRaster** and the BgRaster name are trademarks of Barjonas LLC.

The [Mozilla Public License 2.0](LICENSE) grants rights in copyright and
patents. It grants no rights in the name. This is deliberate and it is normal
practice: it means a copy of BgRaster can be identified as such, and something
that is *not* BgRaster cannot claim to be.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your product, service or installation uses BgRaster, works with
  BgRaster, or is built on BgRaster — as a statement of fact, in ordinary type,
  without implying that it is endorsed by or affiliated with us.
- Use the name in documentation, articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons and
  reviews, favorable or not.
- Keep the name in an unmodified copy you redistribute under the license.
- Use it in the name of a plugin, integration or tool, where the name makes
  clear that yours is the product and BgRaster is what it works with —
  "BgRaster launcher for Foo", not "Foo BgRaster".

## What needs written permission

- Naming a **modified** version "BgRaster", or naming it in a way a reasonable
  person would confuse with BgRaster. If you publish a fork, give it your own
  name. You may say it is derived from BgRaster.
- Using the name in a way that suggests endorsement, affiliation, or official
  status.
- Using it in your company name, product name, domain name, package-manager
  listing or social media handle.
- Merchandise.

## Why this is here

A license controls copying; a trademark controls naming. Without a trademark
notice, a fork could be distributed under a different set of terms while still
calling itself BgRaster, and nobody downstream could tell which one they had.
Asserting the name is what keeps "BgRaster" meaning one thing.
